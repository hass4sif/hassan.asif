About

I'm Hassan Asif, a scholar of digital religion and media. I study what happens when digital technology meets religious life: how people make, edit, share, and remix devotional sound, sermons, and sacred images across platforms, and how that work reshapes doctrine, authority, and aesthetic form.

My work sits where platform studies, sound studies, and digital religion meet, and it moves across sound and image. I work qualitatively, through ethnography, close reading of audiovisual remixes, and media genealogy, with fieldwork across South Asia, the Gulf, East Asia, and Europe.

I also teach, from global copyright to religion in popular culture.

Pirate route8-bit gameCassette delivery

An 8-bit delivery run through Lahore's informal media economy. You are the bootleg cassette itself, reels spinning. Four routes across Lahore, from Hall Road to an Urs night, each one tighter than the last. Drop the reel at every mehfil, masjid, madrasa, shop, dhaba, and mazar. Arrow keys or WASD to move, or use the pad. Deliver all four without getting caught.
